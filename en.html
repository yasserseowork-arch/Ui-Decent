FAQ

Everything you need to know about the store and products

What is Decent and what does it offer?

Decent is a Saudi brand specializing in women’s handbags and travel bags printed with your name or initials. We have served customers across the Kingdom for 4 years, and we offer ready packs that combine a travel bag and a handbag with your name.

How do I personalize my name on a bag?

Choose the bag, size and color, then write the name or letters you want printed — in Arabic or English — printing is free on the signature monogram stripe.

What sizes are available?

Handbags come in several sizes — small and medium are the most requested — and travel bags come in cabin, medium and large sizes with wheels and a handle.

How long does preparing and shipping my order take?

We prepare your order by hand within 3 days, and it reaches you within 3 to 7 days in any city inside the Kingdom. We also ship to the Gulf countries.

How much is shipping and when is it free?

Shipping is free inside the Kingdom for orders above 405 SAR, and the minimum order is 230 SAR.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes — installments are available via Tabby and Tamara at checkout, in addition to Apple Pay, mada, cards, cash on delivery and bank transfer.

What is the exchange & return policy?

The exchange & return policy published on the store’s official policy page applies, noting that name-personalized products are handled as stated there.
